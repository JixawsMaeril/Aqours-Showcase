About Aqours

水の仲間たち — Companions of the Water

Aqours (pronounced "Aqua") is a nine-member school idol group from Uranohoshi Girls' High School in Uchiura, Numazu — a scenic seaside town in Shizuoka Prefecture. Born from the Love Live! Sunshine!! project, they carry on the spirit of their predecessors μ's with a story of friendship, dreams, and saving their beloved school through the power of song and performance.
